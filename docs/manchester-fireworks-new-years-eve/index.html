Manchester fireworks on New Year's Eve 2026

Manchester City Council has put on free midnight fireworks every New Year's Eve since 2023: at Castlefield Bowl for NYE 2023, then at St Peter's Square for NYE 2024 and NYE 2025. Here's what happened last year, what we know about 2026, and the best places to be at midnight.

Is there a Manchester fireworks display for NYE 2026?

Not confirmed yet. Manchester City Council announced the 2025 plans in December 2025, so the 2026 details may not appear until late in the year. We'll update this page as soon as they're published.

What happened on NYE 2025

  • St Peter's Square: a free, alcohol-free, family-friendly countdown from 10pm, hosted by BBC Radio Manchester with a live DJ. An eight-minute fireworks and laser show launched from the roof of Central Library at midnight, with confetti cannons and a soundtrack of Manchester classics. Standing only, with bag searches, and entry stopped once the square was full.
  • Albert Square: open from 10am to 12:30am with the UK's biggest mobile Ferris wheel, fairground rides, food, drink and craft stalls. Free to enter, with rides charged.
  • More than 20,000 people celebrated across the two squares, according to the council.
  • There was no direct walk-through between the two squares, and getting into one did not guarantee entry to the other.

Source: Manchester City Council.

Tips for the city centre squares

Arrive early. St Peter's Square is standing only and closes once it's full.

Pack light. In 2025 bags had to be A4 size or smaller, glass was banned and only guide dogs were allowed in St Peter's Square.

Accessibility. In 2025 there was an accessible viewing area on the Metrolink platform (first come, first served), accessible toilets, and limited accessible parking on Dickinson Street and George Street.

Trams. In 2025 no trams ran through St Peter's Square after 9pm. See getting home.

What to bring

  • Warm layers, a hat and gloves
  • A waterproof: rain is common in late December
  • Charged phone and a power bank
  • Ear defenders for small children
  • A small bag only, and no glass

Fireworks FAQ

Are there fireworks in Manchester on New Year's Eve 2026?

Manchester City Council has run free midnight fireworks for the last three New Year's Eves: at Castlefield Bowl in 2023, then at St Peter's Square in 2024 and 2025, launched from the roof of Central Library. In 2025 the show lasted about eight minutes and more than 20,000 people celebrated across St Peter's Square and Albert Square. The council has not yet confirmed plans for 2026. We'll update our fireworks page as soon as it does.

What time are the Manchester New Year's Eve fireworks?

At midnight. In 2025 the free St Peter's Square event opened at 10pm with a DJ-led countdown, the fireworks and laser show started on the stroke of midnight, and the square closed at about 12:30am. Arrive early, because entry stops once the square is full.

Is there anything free to do in Manchester on New Year's Eve?

Yes. In 2025 the St Peter's Square fireworks and the Albert Square fair (with its giant Ferris wheel) were both free to enter, with rides charged separately. Freight Island's Little Disco and Crazy Pedro's Bridge Street also offered free entry. Look for the "Free" filter in our directory.

What should I wear and bring on New Year's Eve in Manchester?

Dress for cold and probably rain: late-December nights in Manchester are usually only a few degrees above freezing. At the 2025 city events bags had to be A4 or smaller, glass was banned and everyone was searched on entry. Many parties and restaurants have dress codes, so check before you go.

Are the Manchester Christmas Markets open on New Year's Eve?

Mostly not. In 2025 the main Christmas Markets closed on 22 December, but the Albert Square Ferris wheel, fairground rides and stalls, and the Cathedral Gardens ice rink, stayed open until 4 January.
