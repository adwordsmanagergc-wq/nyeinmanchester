NYE Vivaldi Four Seasons by Candlelight

A calm, candlelit start to the night in the medieval cathedral, with Vivaldi and Handel's Royal Fireworks before you head out.

What's included

  • Piccadilly Sinfonietta
  • Violinist Katie Stillman
  • Vivaldi's Four Seasons and Handel's Music for the Royal Fireworks
  • Cathedral lit by hundreds of candles

About NYE Vivaldi Four Seasons by Candlelight

NYE Vivaldi Four Seasons by Candlelight is at Manchester Cathedral, Victoria St in City Centre (Cathedral & Victoria). It's one of the live music options for New Year's Eve 2026 in Manchester. At midnight: Early-evening concert. Age: All ages.
