Raver Tots New Year's Eve Party

The family rave brand brings proper club production to Freight Island for an afternoon countdown. It usually sells out in advance.

What's included

  • Family rave with house, garage and drum & bass
  • Lights, lasers, dancers and effects
  • Face painting and juice for kids
  • Licensed bar for grown-ups
  • Buggy park

About Raver Tots New Year's Eve Party

Raver Tots New Year's Eve Party is at Escape to Freight Island, 11 Baring St in Mayfield (Piccadilly & Mayfield). It's one of the family-friendly, under £30 options for New Year's Eve 2026 in Manchester. At midnight: Daytime countdown. Age: All ages (adults must come with a child).
