LEGOLAND Discovery Centre New Year's Eve Party

A daytime 'midnight' for younger kids, with a DJ, magic and a balloon-drop countdown, all wrapped up by teatime.

What's included

  • Mocktails, crowns and blowers on arrival
  • DJ, magic show and balloon modelling
  • Face painting and character meet-and-greets
  • Rides, 4D cinema and MINILAND
  • Countdown with a balloon drop

About LEGOLAND Discovery Centre New Year's Eve Party

LEGOLAND Discovery Centre New Year's Eve Party is at LEGOLAND Discovery Centre, Trafford Palazzo in Trafford Park (Trafford). It's one of the family-friendly options for New Year's Eve 2026 in Manchester. At midnight: Daytime countdown. Age: Family.
