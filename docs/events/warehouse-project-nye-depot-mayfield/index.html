New Year's Eve at The Warehouse Project

Manchester's biggest NYE club night, confirmed for 2026: Defected and Glitterbox take over the vast former railway depot at Mayfield until 4am.

What's included

  • Defected and Glitterbox stages
  • Eats Everything, Groove Armada, Oden & Fatzo, Mix-Stress
  • Don't F**k with Disco, Melvo Baptiste, Gina Breeze and more
  • Huge warehouse production across multiple rooms
  • Priority access sign-up for tickets

About New Year's Eve at The Warehouse Project

New Year's Eve at The Warehouse Project is at Depot Mayfield, Baring St in Mayfield (Piccadilly & Mayfield). It's one of the club nights, parties options for New Year's Eve 2026 in Manchester. At midnight: Midnight countdown. Age: 18+.
