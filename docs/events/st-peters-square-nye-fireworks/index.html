Manchester NYE Fireworks at St Peter's Square

Manchester City Council's free midnight fireworks drew more than 20,000 people across the city's squares in 2025. Plans for 2026 have not been announced yet.

What's included

  • Midnight fireworks and laser show from the Central Library roof (about 8 minutes in 2025)
  • DJ-led countdown hosted by BBC Radio Manchester
  • Alcohol-free, family-friendly event
  • Hot drinks and snacks
  • Accessible viewing area and toilets

About Manchester NYE Fireworks at St Peter's Square

Manchester NYE Fireworks at St Peter's Square is at St Peter's Square in City Centre (St Peter's Square & Civic Quarter). It's one of the free, family-friendly options for New Year's Eve 2026 in Manchester. At midnight: City fireworks at midnight. Age: All ages.

Planning the rest of your night? Check the timeline and how to get home or read our guide to the city's fireworks and where to watch.
