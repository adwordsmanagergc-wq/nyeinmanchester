About NYE with Our Kids Social at Kargo

NYE with Our Kids Social at Kargo is at Kargo MKT, Central Bay, MediaCity in Salford Quays (Salford & MediaCity). It's one of the family-friendly, under £30 options for New Year's Eve 2026 in Manchester. At midnight: Morning countdown. Age: Family.
