Family-friendly New Year's Eve in Manchester 2026

Manchester's best family NYE plans happen in daylight: countdown parties with balloon drops, family raves and silent discos, all finished by teatime. In 2025 the city's midnight fireworks at St Peter's Square were alcohol-free and family-friendly, but they're late and standing only.

Booking tips

  • Daytime countdowns sell out, so book as soon as tickets are released.
  • Bring ear defenders for babies and toddlers at raves and fireworks.
  • Wrap up warm and pack waterproofs if you'll be outdoors.
  • Agree on a meeting point in case anyone gets separated in the crowds.
